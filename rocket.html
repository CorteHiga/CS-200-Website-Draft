The Space Launch System

The Space Launch System is an expendable two-stage rocket primarily designed to carry astronauts to the Moon in the Orion spacecraft. The rocket has two solid rocket boosters (SRBs), a core stage, and the ICPS upper stage. The vehicle was developed as a replacement for the cancelled Ares I and V rockets after the Constellation program was canncelled. The vehicle also uses a lot of existing hardware from the Space Shuttle program such as the SRBs, the RS-25 engines, etc.
